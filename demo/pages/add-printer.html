Add the office printer to your Mac

IT Help Desk · Updated 2026

Follow these steps to add a printer. You don't need to install a driver for most printers; your Mac finds one automatically.

  1. Open System Settings, then click Printers & Scanners in the sidebar.

  2. Click the Add Printer, Scanner or Fax… button.

  3. Pick your printer from the list and click Add.

  4. To check it was added, open Terminal and run:

    lpstat -p

    You should see your printer's name.
